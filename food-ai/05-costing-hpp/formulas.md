# Costing & HPP — Formula Reference

## Core concepts

### Ingredient cost

`ingredient_cost = quantity_used × purchase_cost_per_unit`

### Recipe cost

`recipe_cost = Σ ingredient_cost + attributable_packaging_cost`

### Portion cost

`portion_cost = total_recipe_cost ÷ number_of_sellable_portions`

### Food cost percentage

`food_cost_% = food_COGS ÷ net_food_sales × 100`

### Beverage cost percentage

`beverage_cost_% = beverage_COGS ÷ net_beverage_sales × 100`

### Gross margin

`gross_margin = net_sales − COGS`

`gross_margin_% = gross_margin ÷ net_sales × 100`

### Contribution margin

`contribution_margin = selling_price − variable_cost_per_unit`

### Break-even units

`break_even_units = fixed_costs ÷ contribution_margin_per_unit`

### Break-even sales

`break_even_sales = fixed_costs ÷ contribution_margin_ratio`

## Mandatory assumptions

Setiap perhitungan harus mencatat:

- currency
- tax treatment
- purchase unit
- recipe unit
- yield
- edible portion
- waste/shrinkage
- packaging
- discounts
- platform commission
- payment fee
- period

## Important distinction

Harga beli bahan tidak selalu sama dengan biaya efektif bahan dalam produk. Yield, waste, trimming, perubahan harga, dan unit conversion dapat mengubah HPP nyata.

## Audit questions

1. Apakah quantity menggunakan unit yang konsisten?
2. Apakah yield benar-benar diukur?
3. Apakah waste dimasukkan?
4. Apakah packaging dimasukkan bila relevan?
5. Apakah harga menggunakan periode yang tepat?
6. Apakah diskon/komisi platform diperlakukan sesuai tujuan analisis?
7. Apakah recipe version tercatat?
8. Apakah hasil perhitungan dapat direproduksi?
