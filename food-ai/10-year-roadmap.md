# Food AI — Roadmap 2026–2036

Food AI diarahkan menjadi knowledge operating system untuk manajemen F&B: pengetahuan → data → keputusan → SOP → eksekusi → evaluasi → pembelajaran.

## Fase

### 2026–2027 — Foundation
- Taxonomy dan knowledge base
- Standarisasi istilah F&B
- SOP dan standard recipe
- HPP, inventory, purchasing, KPI
- Database kasus nyata

### 2028–2029 — Data Layer
- Historical sales
- Inventory movement
- Waste tracking
- Labor productivity
- Supplier performance
- Menu profitability
- Customer behavior

### 2030–2031 — Intelligence Layer
- AI knowledge retrieval
- Root-cause analysis
- Forecasting
- Anomaly detection
- Recommendation engine
- Scenario simulation

### 2032–2033 — Automation Layer
- Automated reporting
- Reorder assistance
- Production planning
- Operational alerts
- Training assistant
- SOP compliance monitoring

### 2034–2036 — F&B Operating Intelligence
Menghubungkan customer, sales, menu, recipe, purchasing, inventory, production, labor, waste, cost, margin, cash flow, decision, dan learning menjadi satu siklus.

## Knowledge domains

Management, operations, menu engineering, recipe standardization, costing/HPP, inventory, purchasing/supply chain, kitchen production, front-of-house service, coffee/beverage, food safety, HR, training, sales/marketing, finance, data analytics, AI/automation, customer experience, multi-outlet expansion, franchise, sustainability, halal compliance, risk management, case studies, templates, SOP, KPI, dan research.

## Prinsip

1. Evidence first.
2. Unit economics first.
3. Operational reality.
4. Data-native.
5. AI sebagai copilot, bukan oracle.
6. Halal, amanah, aman, dan berkelanjutan.
7. Long-term compounding.

## Klasifikasi pengetahuan

- Observed — benar-benar tercatat.
- Calculated — hasil perhitungan.
- Estimated — estimasi dengan asumsi eksplisit.
- Inferred — kesimpulan dari pola.
- Hypothesis — dugaan yang harus diuji.

## Siklus Food AI

Data operasional → pengalaman → pengetahuan → standar → sistem → data baru → pembelajaran.

**Target 2036: dari gudang ilmu menjadi sistem kecerdasan manajemen F&B.**
